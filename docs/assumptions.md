# Assumptions and Open Questions

This is the current validation list for Initiative Tracker. It distinguishes confirmed behavior from unresolved questions and proposals; unknowns and proposals are not confirmed requirements. See [product-requirements.md](product-requirements.md) for context and [product-decisions.md](product-decisions.md) for confirmed decisions.

## Users and workflow

- How workload and pain are distributed among coordinators, initiative owners, decision owners and designated contacts remains to be validated.
- Which coordination activities consume the most effort remains unknown.
- **Confirmed decision-confirmation paths:** The primary path is for the decision owner, or a designated contact acting on the decision owner's behalf, to confirm or update the decision/status directly in the tracker. If the reviewing organization's decision arrives by email, the coordination team may record it manually in the application. Email is optional; automatic email ingestion or status changes are not assumed. After confirmation, the delivery team may update workflow status. Acceptance for implementation maps from **Under review** to **In progress**; a negative decision not to implement sets the local initiative status to **Withdrawn**, meaning it will not be implemented for the defined scope and does not cancel or alter the portfolio-level initiative. This application cannot cancel the portfolio-level initiative; no portfolio-level edit rights or synchronization behavior are implied. No decision within the agreed review period instead triggers escalation. This is confirmed behavior, not MVP approval, and the mapping does not assert that work has started. How the decision is evidenced and how outcomes affect timers and status history remain unresolved. The workflow after further assessment and permissions beyond the named workflow actors remain open. Whether a voluntary local cancellation is a distinct local outcome from a negative implementation decision remains open only if meaningful to the workflow. Health reassessment remains the initiative owner's responsibility; status updating does not grant health-editing ownership.
- After **Under review**, acceptance for implementation maps to **In progress**, and a negative decision not to implement sets the local initiative status to **Withdrawn**. This is local only: it means the initiative will not be implemented for the defined scope and does not cancel or alter the portfolio-level initiative. No decision within the agreed review period instead triggers escalation. Does the workflow differ by initiative type, and what happens after further assessment? **Additional information required** and **Completed** remain proposed values, not an approved sequence. The status mapping does not mean implementation work has already started. Whether a voluntary local cancellation is a distinct local outcome from a negative implementation decision remains open only if meaningful to the workflow.
- **Confirmed archive behavior:** Locally **Withdrawn** initiatives leave the active list and are retained in the same retrievable archive as **Completed** initiatives. This local disposition does not cancel or alter the portfolio-level initiative. The archive must preserve that **Completed** means work has finished, while **Withdrawn** means the initiative will not be implemented for the defined scope; users can filter these outcomes separately. A separate archive feature to display the reason for non-implementation is not needed. This does not approve archive functionality for the MVP.
- How is an initiative represented after the defined decision window expires and escalation occurs? The escalation recipients are confirmed, but the window start, working-day calendar, delivery, automation and subsequent timing remain open.

## Status, timers and freshness

- **Confirmed assessment triggers and accountability:** A new health assessment is prompted during each scheduled update, when the initiative owner reviews and confirms current health and when a material change could affect delivery (for example, a new risk or blocker, a missed milestone, a dependency issue, or resolution of an existing problem). After a material change, the initiative owner is responsible for reviewing its impact and submitting an updated health assessment. The decision owner is the primary and only confirmed stakeholder who may flag a material change or raise a concern to prompt owner review. The initiative owner owns assessment and is not an additional stakeholder flagger; the internal coordination team is not a material-change flagger. A flag prompts owner review only and does not itself change health; the initiative owner remains accountable for reviewing the impact and submitting an updated assessment. Only an explicit assessment changes health. If no new assessment is submitted, retain the last reported health and track update freshness independently. Automatic detection of every material event is not assumed.
- **Confirmed behavior:** Workflow stage and health are independent. Changing an initiative's workflow stage must not automatically change its health; health changes only when a new assessment of the initiative's condition is made. Health remains at its last reported value (for example, **On track**) when update freshness becomes **Overdue**. An overdue update must not automatically change health to **At risk** or **Blocked**; missing information and delivery problems are different. The interface must identify health as coming from the last submitted update and show when that update was received, so stale health is not presented as verified current. **Overdue** is a confirmed freshness label, not a health value. **Awaiting update** is neither a health value nor a confirmed freshness label.
- **Highest-priority unresolved questions:** How is a material-change flag submitted and how does it reach or get surfaced to the initiative owner? The decision owner is the primary and only confirmed stakeholder flagger; the initiative owner owns assessment and is not an additional stakeholder flagger, and the internal coordination team is not a flagger. The notification mechanism and technical implementation (including data model, interface mechanics and business logic) remain open; do not assume email or automatic routing. Access controls for submitting and viewing flags also remain unresolved. The initiative owner remains accountable for reviewing the impact and submitting an updated health assessment; a flag prompts that review but does not change health. For decision confirmation, the primary tracker-update path and optional manual coordination team recording of email-sourced decisions are confirmed, with no automatic email ingestion or status change. Acceptance maps from **Under review** to **In progress**; a negative decision not to implement sets the local initiative status to **Withdrawn**, meaning it will not be implemented for the defined scope and does not cancel or alter the portfolio-level initiative. This application cannot cancel the portfolio-level initiative; no portfolio-level edit rights or synchronization behavior are implied. No decision within the agreed review period instead triggers escalation. The mapping does not assert that work has started. Decision evidence, authorization beyond named workflow actors and timer/history behavior remain unresolved, as does the workflow after further assessment. Whether a voluntary local cancellation is a distinct local outcome from a negative implementation decision remains open only if meaningful to the workflow. The exact health value list and how an assessment is submitted, stored and displayed also remain open. Exact workflow stages are not approved; exact freshness labels other than **Overdue**, the cutoff and what clears the overdue state also remain unresolved.
- When does the defined decision window begin, and how are local holidays and working-day calendars handled?
- What happens after a missed decision is escalated, and what are the escalation details, delivery method and automation? The decision owner and internal coordination team are confirmed recipients.
- Which exact update cutoff and time zone apply? What qualifies as an update, and what clears the **Overdue**/red freshness indicator?
- How do the overall-condition and specific-stage dimensions map to the timers and history? Does changing only the specific progress detail reset the current-status timer?
- The total elapsed-time start is unresolved: the initial brief says first active status, while later discovery refers to the initiative being sent/submitted to the reviewing organization. Its terminal outcomes are resolved: the timer stops on either **Completed** or locally **Withdrawn**, and its final value is retained in the archive for both.
- Does the separate current-status duration timer also stop on local **Withdrawn**, and should its final value be retained? Do not infer this from the confirmed total elapsed-time behavior.
- No health value list is approved. Examples such as **On track**, **At risk**, **Blocked** and **Completed** remain proposals. **Overdue** is the confirmed freshness label; **Awaiting update** is not a confirmed health value or freshness label.
