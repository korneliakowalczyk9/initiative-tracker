# Product Decisions

This is the log of confirmed product decisions for Initiative Tracker. Proposed status values, unvalidated details and MVP priorities are not decisions. See [product-requirements.md](product-requirements.md) for the full product brief and requirements.

## Confirmed decisions

- Build a standalone tool focused on initiatives relevant to the specific organizational scope; it is not limited to regulatory work.
- The project remains in discovery. Do not begin application implementation until the initial product requirements and MVP scope are agreed.
- Keep overall initiative condition separate from the specific workflow stage.
- Use **New** for a created initiative that has not yet been submitted to the reviewing organization, followed by **Under review**. Do not use **To do** as a separate status.
- While an initiative is **Under review**, the reviewing organization has the agreed review period to decide whether it will be implemented. If no decision is made within that window, escalate to the decision owner and the internal coordination team. A missed decision alone does not mean the initiative is **Blocked**.
- **Under review** is the decision-review stage. If the reviewing organization accepts the initiative for implementation, the next specific status is **In progress**. This status mapping does not assert that implementation work has already started. If the reviewing organization decides not to implement, the local initiative status is **Withdrawn**: it will not be implemented for the defined scope and does not cancel or alter the portfolio-level initiative. This application cannot cancel the portfolio-level initiative; no portfolio-level edit rights or synchronization behavior are implied. This is distinct from no decision within the defined review window, which triggers escalation.
- The primary decision-confirmation path is for the decision owner, or a designated contact acting on the decision owner's behalf, to confirm or update the decision/status directly in the tracker. If the decision arrives by email, the coordination team may record it manually in the application; email is optional, and automatic email ingestion or status changes are not assumed. After confirmation, the delivery team may update workflow status. This confirms the method, not the workflow after further assessment. Whether a voluntary local cancellation is a distinct local outcome from a negative implementation decision remains open only if meaningful to the workflow. Permissions beyond the named workflow actors and how acceptance or withdrawal affect timers and status history remain open. It does not approve MVP scope.
- The total elapsed-time timer stops when an initiative is either **Completed** or locally **Withdrawn**, and its final value is retained in the archive for either outcome. The timer's start point remains unresolved: first active status versus the event of sending/submitting to the reviewing organization. This confirms behavior, not MVP approval.
- Locally **Withdrawn** initiatives leave the active list and are retained in the same retrievable archive as **Completed** initiatives. **Withdrawn** means the initiative will not be implemented for the defined scope; it is a local disposition only and does not cancel or alter the portfolio-level initiative. The archive must retain the distinct outcomes of **Completed** and **Withdrawn**, and users can filter these outcomes separately. This confirms archive behavior, not MVP approval; the archive's exact layout remains open.
- A separate archive feature to display the reason the reviewing organization decided not to implement an initiative is not needed.
- Status updates follow an agreed cadence and deadline. If the required update is not received by the agreed deadline, the record turns red to indicate that progress is unknown and needs follow-up, not that the initiative is blocked.
- Decision owners should be able to update the status of their respective initiatives. Their broader edit permissions remain undecided.

## Scope status and discovery prototype authorization

- The product owner authorized a discovery-only clickable prototype for the confirmed **New** → **Under review** → **In progress** or **Withdrawn** status outcomes, using fictional data and no backend or saved data. It is a standalone artifact under `UI/prototype/`, separate from future application implementation. This authorization does not approve the MVP, requirements, or a technical stack. Timer behavior, further-assessment mapping, escalation automation and permission enforcement are not implemented; unresolved follow-up may be shown only as a nonfunctional note.

These decisions do not approve the full requested feature list or any feature for the MVP. Exact overall health values, workflow after further assessment, authorization scope, timing details and timer/history behavior remain subject to validation. Whether a voluntary local cancellation is a distinct local outcome from a negative implementation decision remains open only if meaningful to the workflow.
